# The Data Analytics Journey

## Course Overview

The Data Analytics Journey introduces the analytics life cycle and its application to real-world business challenges. Students learn how to gather requirements, ask relevant business questions, establish evaluation metrics, and align project outcomes with organizational goals.

The course also highlights the different domains of data analytics — including data analysis, data engineering, and decision process engineering — while providing exposure to potential career paths and specializations in the field.

This foundation equips learners to approach analytics work with both a strategic business mindset and a practical data-driven approach.
## Key Learnings

Through this course and its assignments, I gained experience in:

- Applying the Analytics Life Cycle
Used the structured process to frame business problems, analyze data, and propose evidence-based solutions.

- Developing Data Analytics Plans
Designed end-to-end analytics plans to address specific business problems, including defining objectives, metrics, and deliverables.

- Exploring Career Pathways
Differentiated between various careers and roles across analytics, engineering, and decision science.

- Career Alignment & Planning
Identified personal career goals within data analytics, supported by foundational exposure to industry pathways.

Skills gained in this course can be applied to:
